# Messaging Pillars · Roamly Groups

## 1. From/To market shift

- **From, life before:** Finding a local experience online was easy. Getting the group from “let’s do it” to a confirmed booking was still one friend’s job. They had to find a time that worked in the group chat, keep track of who was in, and either juggle separate reservations or pay upfront and chase everyone for their share. Sometimes the group gave up before anyone booked.
- **To, life after:** Booking a local experience becomes something friends do together. They agree on a time, each person pays their share, and everyone knows where to find the confirmed plan. The friend who starts the booking can look forward to the experience without covering the whole bill or chasing people to pay them back.

## 2. Audiences

- **Number of audiences that need their own message:** 1
- **Why you picked this number:** I picked one audience because the organizer is the person who decides how the group will book. They’re also the one juggling plans and chasing payments, so they have the strongest reason to try Roamly Groups. Invited friends only need to know how to join and pay their share, which the invitation can explain.

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Audience 1: Existing Roamly customers who are usually the friend in charge of booking local experiences.
- **Value prop:** Get the group booked without covering the whole bill or chasing friends for their share. Spend less time organizing and more time enjoying the experience with them.
- **Capabilities:** One group booking: The organizer starts a booking for the experience and invites friends to join.
Split payments: Each friend pays their share directly toward the same booking.
Shared itinerary: Everyone can see the confirmed time, meeting point, and booking details in one place.
- **Evidence:** One group booking: Measure how often groups go from invitation to confirmed booking. Compare organizer time and effort with how they booked before.
Split payments: Track how often everyone pays their share directly and how often organizers still have to cover an unpaid balance or send reminders.
Shared itinerary: Test whether friends can find the confirmed time and meeting point without asking the organizer.

## 4. Copy across three surfaces

- **Onboarding tooltip:** Invite your friends. Each person pays their share toward this booking.
- **Social post:** Always the friend who books the activity and chases everyone to pay you back? With Roamly Groups, you can start one booking and let each friend pay their share directly. Once it’s confirmed, everyone can find the time and meeting point in one place.
- **In-app notification:** Going with friends? Start a group booking for this experience and let everyone pay their own share.
