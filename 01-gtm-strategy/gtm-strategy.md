# GTM Strategy · Roamly Groups

## 1. Discover Framework

### Align on the Goal
- **Core business problem:** Roamly is missing out on bookings because planning an experience as a group takes too much effort. People already want to book together, but juggling separate accounts, splitting payments manually, and finding a time that works for everyone causes many groups to give up before booking.
Roamly Groups aims to make that process easier, helping more groups complete their bookings and increasing revenue for the business.
- **What success looks like:** If the launch goes well, booking a group experience on Roamly becomes easier, with fewer people dropping off because of coordination or payment issues. Customers feel heard because Roamly has acted on their feedback and solved a real frustration. This strengthens the brand’s reputation, builds trust, and gives customers more reasons to return and recommend Roamly to others, helping grow the customer base over time.

### Do the Research
- **Signals of a real opportunity:** People are already finding ways to book group experiences on Roamly, even though the process is difficult. They’re using separate accounts, splitting payments themselves, and coordinating schedules outside the platform. That’s a strong sign that the demand already exists.
The high drop-off rates and repeated support feedback suggest that the booking process is getting in their way. Roamly’s strong repeat booking rate also shows that customers enjoy the experiences once they book.
This makes group booking an opportunity worth exploring. I’d want to understand how many groups face these issues and where they give up, then test whether making the process easier helps more of them complete a booking.

## 5. V1 GTM strategy one-pager

### Roamly Groups: V1 GTM Strategy

**Primary goal: Expand**  
Help existing customers book more experiences with friends, increasing total booking value. Acquiring new customers through invitations is a secondary benefit.

**Target segment**  
Existing Roamly customers organizing experiences with friends, starting with those who have reported group booking difficulties or shown group booking intent.

**Customer problem**  
Coordinating separate bookings, schedules, and payments creates extra work for organizers and causes groups to abandon their plans.

**Value proposition**  
Roamly Groups lets friends plan and book local experiences together, split payments, and share an itinerary—without chasing everyone for money.

**GTM motion: PLG-led hybrid, with a community test**  
The main growth mechanism is a self-serve flow: one friend starts a group booking and invites others to join and pay. Travel communities already contribute to Roamly’s discovery, so we’ll test their ability to drive Groups adoption before committing to a broader community-led motion.

**Recommended channel approach**

- **In-product:** Introduce Groups on relevant experience pages and make invitations easy to send and join.
- **Targeted email:** Reach existing customers with known group intent, using a clear “Plan your next experience with friends” call to action.
- **Travel communities:** Pilot with a small number of engaged communities. Enable members to share real group experiences and practical planning tips, using trackable links to measure completed bookings.

**Launch approach**  
Start in a few cities with hosts who can accommodate groups. Test the full journey, including payment deadlines, cancellations, and refunds, before expanding.

**Success metric**  
Measure **incremental net booking value per eligible existing customer** versus a randomized control group over the pilot period, counting both individual and group bookings to detect substitution.

Supporting measures: group booking completion, invitation-to-paid-participant conversion, and community-referred completed bookings. Monitor cancellations, payment failures, and support contacts.

**Core hypothesis**  
If we make organizing and paying for shared experiences easier, existing customers will complete additional bookings with friends. Community recommendations may help more customers discover and try the feature.

**Next step**  
Validate group intent and host readiness, establish baseline metrics, and set a pilot target before launch.
